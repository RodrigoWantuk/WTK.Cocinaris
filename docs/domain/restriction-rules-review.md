## What this does

Every dish card from the AI or the shared idea library is checked against the person's confirmed restrictions before it is shown, and the full recipe is checked again after expansion. The model is told about restrictions, but its output is untrusted, so this check does not depend on it.

The check helps but does not replace reading labels. The interface says so.

## The three outcomes

| Outcome | When | What the person sees |
|---|---|---|
| **Blocked** | An ingredient breaks a rule | The dish is not shown. Saved recipes are hidden from this plan only and stay in the library. |
| **Unverified** | The rule cannot vouch for an ingredient, or the restriction has no rule | The dish is shown with: "Confira: não consegui verificar {ingredient} para {restriction}." |
| **Allowed** | Nothing objects | The dish is shown. |

An ingredient is matched in two ways:

- by catalog category and ingredient, when its name is in the catalog;
- by the keywords below, on whole words, ignoring case and accents, and accepting a plural ending. Exceptions are removed first.

Under an allergy, intolerance, or medical restriction, an ingredient the catalog does not know and no keyword matches is **Unverified**. Under a dietary pattern, it is **Allowed**.

## Restrictions that cannot be checked

No word list can express these, so **every** dish shows the note, and the setup screen says: "Não consigo conferir '{restriction}' automaticamente. Confira os ingredientes."

`keto`, `paleo`, `low_carb`, `mediterranean_diet`, `fructose_intolerance`, `histamine_intolerance`, `low_sodium_diet`, `low_sugar_diet`, `renal_diet`, `low_fodmap_diet`, `heart_healthy_diet`, `diabetic_friendly_diet`

A custom restriction the person typed, and the curated religious and ethical codes (`halal`, `kosher`, `hindu_vegetarian`, `jain_diet`, `lent_fasting`, `buddhist_vegetarian`, `cruelty_free_only`, `sustainable_seafood_only`, `fair_trade_only`, `local_sourcing_preferred`, `no_palm_oil`), behave the same way. `vegan_ethical` has a rule, the same as `vegan`.

## Dislikes never block

A dislike only moves a dish down the deck. Curated dislikes: `cilantro`, `mushrooms`, `olives`, `blue_cheese`, `liver`, `raw_onion`, `licorice`, `anchovies`.

## Known limits, for the owner to accept or change

- **Words can miss.** A dish named freely by the model, with an ingredient spelled in an unexpected way, may pass under a dietary pattern. Allergies and intolerances show a note in that case instead.
- **Words can overblock.** "massa" blocks pasta under gluten rules, so "massa folhada" is hidden too, correctly, but so might a name not covered by the exceptions.
- **Hidden ingredients are not known.** "Caldo" and bouillon may contain meat or gluten, so they are marked unverified under those rules. Other processed foods are not detected.
- **Cross-contamination is not considered.** Oats under gluten rules are marked unverified for this reason; nothing else is.
