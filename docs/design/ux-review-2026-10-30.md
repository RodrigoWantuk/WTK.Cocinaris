# UX and Design Review — 2026-10-30

- **Plan:** [PLAN-0057](../plans/PLAN-0057-registry-and-ux-redesign.md) Phase 1
- **Build:** `main` at `99481b8` plus documentation-only commits.
- **Method:** Every production route in `app/ProductionApp.tsx` (19 routes plus the account menu) was captured through the proxy replica (`scripts/e2e/proxy-replica.sh`) at 390 × 844 and 1280 × 900, in the light theme and with the `.dark` class forced. The data was a realistic pantry: frozen chicken, items near their date, and estimated amounts. It also had two recipes, an accepted menu with meals today and tomorrow, and a cooking session in progress. axe-core 4 (WCAG 2.0/2.1/2.2 A and AA plus best practices) ran on each route. The screens were then reviewed against the owner's new design direction (PLAN-0057, "Owner decision") and the everyday-cook persona.
- **Not checked:** a screen-reader pass, 200% zoom, and the English and Spanish catalogs on every screen. The Phase 2 checks cover them.

## Summary

The product works end to end, and automated accessibility is clean: axe found no violations on any route. Touch targets, labels, and status-in-words rules from the 2026-10-29 system hold. The main problems are of three kinds:

- **Language.** Several screens speak like the specification ("gateway de IA", "candidatos validados", "inventário", "reconciliados"). The pantry item page reads like an admin form.
- **Hierarchy.** "Hoje" stacks boxed sections of equal weight. The menu repeats the same two buttons eight times.
- **Visual system.** Black is the only accent. There is no hover or press feedback. Dark-mode tokens exist, but production cannot reach them.

The owner's new direction (layered neutral surfaces, an emerald accent, short micro-interactions) addresses the third group directly. The first two groups need screen-level changes.

Keep: the bottom bar with the central "+", the row-based lists, the guided cooking flow (preparation, one step per screen, one-tap finish), the store-section shopping list, the dual measures, and the advisory wording on dates.

## Findings

### H1. "O que cozinho agora?" speaks like the system — High
- **Where:** `features/recipes/RecipeGeneratePage.tsx` and its catalog keys.
- **What happens:** The page says "O Cocinaris lerá a despensa e as preferências e pedirá ao gateway de IA exatamente três candidatos validados" and the button is "Pedir três candidatos". The guidance options are hidden in a closed disclosure, and the rest of the phone screen is empty.
- **Why it matters:** This is the app's signature action, and "gateway" and "candidatos" are internal terms.
- **Fix:** Use plain copy ("Vamos sugerir 3 receitas com o que você tem"). Name the button "Sugerir receitas". Show the guidance as visible chips (meal, time, cuisine) with "Mais opções" for required and excluded items, and use the empty space for what will be considered (items to use soon, restrictions).

### H2. Recipe ingredients say "comprar ou já ter em casa" even when the pantry has them — High
- **Where:** `features/recipes/RecipeContent.tsx` (ingredient source label).
- **What happens:** Every ingredient of a manual recipe shows "comprar ou já ter em casa". This includes chicken, rice, and onion, which are in the pantry.
- **Why it matters:** The core promise is to cook with what you have. This line contradicts it and hides what is missing.
- **Fix:** Show availability from the pantry, using the same matching as the cooking proposal: "Na despensa", "Faltam 100 g", or "Não está na despensa". Fall back to no label when the pantry cannot be read.

### H3. The pantry item page reads like an admin form — High
- **Where:** `features/inventory/ProductionInventoryDetail.tsx`.
- **What happens:**
  - "Editar" and a red "Excluir" of the same size come first.
  - Five fields follow, most of them "Nenhum".
  - Then three boxed technical forms: "Mudar estado" (an "Ação" select), "Reservas e proteção" (radios), and "Ajustar quantidade" (a "Tipo de ajuste" select).
- **Why it matters:** The everyday actions (used some, opened it, froze it, threw it away) need two selects and a submit.
- **Fix:**
  - Lead with quantity, place, and date status.
  - Offer task buttons: "Usei", "Abri", "Congelei", "Descongelar", "Joguei fora". Each opens a small sheet asking only for the amount.
  - Hide empty fields.
  - Move reservations and protection into a disclosure.
  - Make delete a quiet action at the bottom with confirmation.

### H4. Dark mode cannot be reached in production — High (new direction)
- **Where:** `index.css` has `.dark` tokens, but only the prototype store toggles the class. `ProductionApp` honors neither `prefers-color-scheme` nor a setting.
- **What happens:** People who use a dark system theme get the light app. The dark tokens are untested in production.
- **Fix:**
  - Follow the system theme by default.
  - Add "Tema: Sistema, Claro, Escuro" to the account menu, stored per device.
  - Verify contrast of both themes with the new tokens.

### H5. Visual language does not match the chosen direction — High (owner decision)
- **Where:** `index.css`, `tailwind.config.js`, `components/ui/*`, and section containers.
- **What happens:**
  - Black is the primary color everywhere.
  - Focus rings are neutral.
  - There is no hover or press feedback.
  - Every section on "Hoje" is the same white bordered box, so nothing leads.
- **Fix:** Phase 2 design system:
  - an emerald accent for the primary CTA, active and selected state, and focus ring;
  - neutral layered surfaces (background, then card, then elevated) on a Zinc scale;
  - subtle borders, and `ring-1 ring-white/5` in dark;
  - hover lift or tint and press scale at 150–200 ms, respecting reduced motion;
  - tabular numbers for quantities.

  The green "you have it" status keeps its own token and text label.

### H6. Public entry page uses product-spec language and shows placeholders — High
- **Where:** `features/entry/PublicEntryPage.tsx` and its catalog.
- **What happens:**
  - The page says "inventário", "possibilidades de preparo explicáveis", and "consumo, sobras, congelamento e desperdício são reconciliados".
  - The step cards are numbered 01–05 like a spec.
  - The footer says "Termos (texto jurídico em preparação)" and "Links jurídicos são placeholders".
- **Why it matters:** It is the first impression, and placeholder legal text reads as unfinished.
- **Fix:** Rewrite in the cook's words (what you get in one sentence; three short benefits), and add one visual of the app. Show legal links only when the documents exist, or label them "em breve" without the word "placeholder".

### M1. "Hoje" hierarchy — Medium
- **What happens:**
  - "Você está cozinhando" sits third, below the menu and "Prepare-se".
  - "O que cozinho agora?" repeats the page question in a box.
  - "Use logo" repeats the pantry attention box.
  - Each reminder shows "Hoje" twice: the chip and "· Hoje".
- **Fix:** Order the sections as:
  1. cooking in progress (resume);
  2. today's menu;
  3. "Prepare-se";
  4. the main action, as a single strong card;
  5. "Use logo";
  6. recipes.

  Drop the duplicate day label in reminders.

### M2. Menu page is long and repetitive — Medium
- **What happens:**
  - Every day card repeats "Adicionar refeição" and "Sugerir com IA", eight pairs on the page.
  - The remove "×" sits alone on its own row with no visible label.
  - Missing ingredients are in destructive red.
- **Fix:**
  - Show compact empty days: a single "+ Adicionar" that opens a sheet with "Da biblioteca" and "Sugerir com IA".
  - Move remove into the meal's actions with a visible label.
  - Use the warning tone for "Falta".

### M3. Pantry list filters crowd the top — Medium
- **What happens:**
  - Search, two native selects, a "Buscar" button, and "Limpar filtros" come before the list.
  - The attention box duplicates rows that appear again below.
  - Large amounts show as "1.000 g" instead of "1 kg".
- **Fix:**
  - Make search live with a debounce.
  - Turn status and place into chips.
  - Make the attention box a filter chip ("Usar logo (2)") instead of a duplicate list.
  - Format large metric amounts in kg or L.

### M4. Account button shows "?" — Medium
- **Where:** `app/shell/AccountMenu.tsx`.
- **What happens:** Without a display name, the avatar is "?", which reads as Help.
- **Fix:** Fall back to a person icon with the accessible name "Conta".

### M5. Profile overview repeats itself — Medium
- **What happens:** "Você ainda não começou", "0% completo", "Próximos passos" (three items), and then three section cards with the same three items.
- **Fix:** One intro line plus the three section rows, each with its status ("Não preenchido", "2 restrições").

### M6. Bottom navigation stays visible while cooking — Medium
- **Where:** guided cooking (`CookingPage.tsx`).
- **What happens:** The tab bar competes with "Começar" and the step controls, and a tap can leave the session.
- **Fix:** Hide the bottom bar in the guided cooking route and keep a clear "Sair do preparo" in the header.

### M7. Shopping list noise — Medium
- **What happens:** "Informar tamanho do pacote" appears on every line, and a three-sentence paragraph explains the list.
- **Fix:** Move the package size into the "Comprei" sheet and a line overflow. Shorten the intro to one sentence with a "Como funciona" disclosure.

### M8. Recipe page labels and actions — Medium
- **What happens:**
  - The eyebrow says "Versão 1".
  - The page says "Tipos de refeição:" and "0 min de espera".
  - Four secondary pills compete under the main action, plus "Arquivar".
  - A "Voltar" button sits at the bottom.
- **Fix:**
  - Show the version only in history.
  - Show meal types as chips.
  - Hide zero waits.
  - Keep "Favoritar" as an icon button and put "Editar", "Criar variação", and "Arquivar" in an overflow menu.
  - Remove the bottom "Voltar".

### L1. Date badges are wordy — Low
"Use hoje ou amanhã · Usar até 31 de out. de 2026" repeats the idea and shows the year for near dates. Write "Usar até amanhã" or "Usar até sex., 31 out."

### L2. Native selects — Low
The unit, status, place, and action selects use the native look, unlike the pills and chips. Use segmented chips where there are four options or fewer, and a styled select otherwise.

### L3. Recipe cards without a photo — Low
The generic placeholder icon and the narrow title column make titles wrap three lines on phones. Use a tinted surface with the dish initial or meal icon, and give the title the full width when there is no image.

## Suggested order

1. **Phase 2:**
   - H5 and H4 (design tokens, themes, components), because every screen depends on them;
   - then H1, H2, H3, and H6;
   - then M1 and M4, which are small and on the most-used screen.
2. **Phase 3:** M2, M3, M5, M6, M7, M8, and L1–L3.

## Resolution

- **Phase 2 ([PR #72](https://github.com/RodrigoWantuk/WTK.Cocinaris/pull/72)):** H1, H2, H4, H5, H6, M1, and M4 resolved; H3 in part.
- **Phase 3:**
  - **H3 and L2:** action types, places, states, and availability are chips (`ChoiceChips`) instead of selects.
  - **M2:** the days sit on one surface; add and suggest are compact icon buttons; remove is a labelled trash icon; "Falta" uses the warning tone.
  - **M3:** search is live (300 ms) and places are chips. Clear appears only when a filter is set, and large amounts show in kg or L. The attention box is kept as is, because no filter for it exists in the API.
  - **M5:** one status block with a progress bar, and section rows that show "Não preenchido".
  - **M6:** the bottom bar is hidden while cooking, with an exit link.
  - **M7:** the package size appears while buying or when one is set, and the intro is one sentence.
  - **M8:** no version eyebrow; meal types are chips; zero waits are hidden; favorite is an icon button; the less frequent actions sit under "Mais"; the bottom "Voltar" is gone.
  - **L1:** short use-by dates, with no repeated "hoje ou amanhã".
  - **L3:** a recipe without a photo shows the dish initial on the accent tint.
