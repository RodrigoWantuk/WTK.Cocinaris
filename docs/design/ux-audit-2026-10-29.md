# UX and Design-System Audit — 2026-10-29

- **Plan:** [PLAN-0054](../plans/PLAN-0054-everyday-cook-realignment.md)
- **Method:** Every production screen was captured at 390 × 844 (phone) and 1280 × 900 (desktop) through the proxy replica, with a realistic pantry, recipes, an accepted menu, and a cooking session in progress. It was then reviewed against the persona in [`everyday-cook-realignment-2026-10-29.md`](../product/everyday-cook-realignment-2026-10-29.md).

## Defects found (fixed in the PLAN-0054 Phase 0 PR)

- **Pantry items missed in the cooking review.** The completion review said eggs and tomatoes "did not come from the pantry" although lots with those names existed. Only recipes generated from a specific lot were matched. The proposal now matches by normalized name and unit, the same way as the shopping list and reservations (`RecipeIngredientReader.NormalizeName`).
- **Stale menu copy.** The menu's "in use" hint still said nothing is reserved in the pantry, which stopped being true when PLAN-0029 Phase 3 merged.

## Findings

### Navigation and layout
- **Header.** On a phone the header wraps into two lines (Perfil drops to a second row). The language selector and "Sair" take the space of primary destinations.
- **Navigation placement.** There is no bottom navigation, so destinations sit at the top, far from the thumb.
- **Main action.** Several screens have no clear single main action; three to five buttons of similar weight compete (recipe page: Cozinhar, Favoritar, Editar, Criar variação, Arquivar).

### Visual language
- **Muted, low-energy palette.** Cream ground, grey-green primary, serif display type. It reads as editorial and calm, not as food or as a tool for the kitchen.
- **Small type and targets.** Body and controls are small for a phone in the kitchen: 12–14 px detail text, 40 px controls, and default native selects in many places.
- **Flat hierarchy.** Every card has the same border and weight, so attention items, the planned meal, and the main suggestion look alike.
- **State by text only.** Status is mostly text, with no consistent icon-plus-word pill.

### Pantry
- **"Lote" everywhere.** "Adicionar lote", "Buscar lotes", "Tipo de quantidade: Medida / Qualitativa" are database words.
- **The add form is a long page.** Name, quantity type, quantity, unit, place, package state, printed date in `YYYY-MM-DD`, and notes. No suggestions, and no way to add several items in a row.
- **Approximate amounts are second-class.** Lots without a number are ignored by suggestions, menus, reservations, and shopping.
- **The item page is a stack of advanced panels.** Change state, Reserve and protect, Adjust quantity, History. Everything shows at once.

### Recipes and cooking
- **Metric units only.** Ingredients show grams, millilitres, or units. There are no household measures and no "you have it" indication; "(comprar ou já ter em casa)" is shown for items that are in the pantry.
- **Cooking is a long checklist.** Every stage on one page, with no step-by-step mode, no timer, and no help.
- **Completion is a form.** It asks for checkboxes, item selectors, quantities, leftovers, rating, and notes right after cooking.

### Menu and shopping
- **Too many buttons per day.** Each day shows two buttons even when empty, and each meal has four actions (Cozinhar, Pular, Mudar, ✕).
- **The shopping list belongs to one menu.** It is grouped by internal categories ("Já tem", "Opcionais") instead of store sections. A purchase is recorded one item at a time through a form.
- **No shared list for a household.**

### Onboarding
- **New accounts land on an empty "Hoje".** The profile is 40 % complete, there is no guided first step, and nothing asks how many people eat or what is at home.

## Proposed direction

The direction is drawn on the design canvas linked from PLAN-0054, with the system and five phone screens: Hoje, Despensa with quick add, Receita with both measures, Cozinhar step by step, and Compras for the household.

- **Look.** A warm white ground, a tomato main action, herb green for "you have it", saffron for attention, and one sans family (Figtree) at 17 px body.
- **Controls.** Touch targets of 48 px or more, state always as an icon plus a word, and one filled button per screen.
- **Structure.** Bottom navigation (Hoje, Despensa, Receitas, Cardápio, Compras), with the profile, household, and language in the avatar.
- **Interaction.** Sheets instead of form pages, and advanced features behind "more" or shown only on conflict.
